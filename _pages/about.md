I am an **Associate Professor** and **Head of the Department of Computer and Software Engineering** at [Ariel University](https://www.ariel.ac.il), Israel. I also serve as **Head of the Ariel Cyber Innovation Center (ACIC)**.

My research focuses on the intersection of **machine learning and networking**, with an emphasis on:
- **Encrypted traffic classification** — understanding network flows without breaking encryption
- **Network security** — anomaly detection, intrusion detection, and MANET protection
- **Quality of Experience (QoE)** — modeling and predicting user experience in video streaming and online gaming
- **Adaptive video streaming** — HTTP Adaptive Streaming (HAS) optimization

I received my B.Sc., M.Sc., and Ph.D. from **Ben-Gurion University of the Negev**, all in Communication Systems Engineering. From 2011–2012, I was a Postdoctoral Fellow at the [Laboratory of Cryptography and System Security (CrySyS Lab)](https://www.crysys.hu/), Budapest University of Technology and Economics.

I lead a research group including PhD and M.Sc. students, and collaborate closely with industry and national research programs. I have over **100 publications** in leading journals and conferences.
