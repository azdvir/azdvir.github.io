Mobile Ad Hoc Networks (MANETs) operate without fixed infrastructure, making them invaluable for military and emergency scenarios — but uniquely vulnerable. This project investigates blackhole/wormhole attacks, Sybil spoofing, and denial-of-service, alongside trust-based routing protocols and lightweight cryptographic defenses suited to resource-constrained nodes.
